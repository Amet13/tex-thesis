\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

% Report what was measured, how often, and the spread, not only the best run.
\Cref{fig:accuracy} shows validation accuracy per epoch; the numbers come from \path{figures/data/results.csv}.
\Cref{tab:main} summarizes the final epoch.
Following~\cite{hoefler2015scientific}, every number is the mean over $n = 5$ runs with different random seeds, reported with its standard deviation.
All numbers in this template are sample data.
