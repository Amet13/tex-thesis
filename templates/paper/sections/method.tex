\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

% Define notation once, then describe the method precisely enough to reproduce.
Let $\mathcal{D} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^{n}$ be a training set.
We minimize the regularized empirical risk
\begin{equation}\label{eq:objective}
  \hat{\theta} = \arg\min_{\theta} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \ell\bigl(f_\theta(x_i), y_i\bigr) + \lambda \lVert \theta \rVert_2^2 ,
\end{equation}
where $\ell$ is the cross-entropy loss and $\lambda \ge 0$ controls regularization.
Parameters are updated with Adam~\cite{kingma2014adam} at learning rate $\eta = \num{3e-4}$.
